# Blinkit-Sales-Analysis-PowerBI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Blinkit sales data using Power BI to understand sales performance, products, categories, outlets, and monthly trends.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query


## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Top Selling Product
- Average Rating

## Analysis Performed

- Sales by product category
- Sales by outlet location
- Monthly sales trends
- Product-level analysis

## Skills Demonstrated

- ETL (Extract, Transform, Load)
- Data Cleaning
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Development

  
## Key Insights

- **Overall Performance:** The dashboard records total sales of approximately **1.36M** across around **5K orders**, with an average customer rating of **4.25**.

- **Top Product Category:** **Personal Care** generated the highest category sales at approximately **260.5K**, followed by **Bakery (232.2K)** and **Household (226.86K)**.

- **Lowest-Performing Category:** **Snacks** recorded the lowest sales among the displayed categories at approximately **64.2K**, followed by **Vegetables (83.3K)**.

- **Top Sales City:** **Satara** recorded the highest sales among the top 10 cities at approximately **149.94K**, followed by **Solapur (140.96K)** and **Pune (140.37K)**.

- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Sales peaked in **March at approximately 132K**, while the lowest monthly sales were recorded in **June at approximately 92K**.

- **Store Type Performance:** Sales were relatively evenly distributed across the three store types: **Express (459.6K)**, **Superstore (452.75K)** and **Mini (445.15K)**.

- **Top Selling Product:** **Cake** is shown as the top-selling product in the dashboard's KPI section.

- **Product-Level Performance:** Among the displayed top 10 products, **Detergent 1kg** had the highest quantity/value shown (219), followed by **Shampoo 180ml (214)** and **Face Wash (198)**.
